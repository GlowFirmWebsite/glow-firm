THE SCIENCE OF THE GLOW

Charged by light.
Made to stand out.

01

Absorb

Photoluminescent pigments absorb energy from suitable light sources.

02

Store

Energy is temporarily stored in the pigment's luminescent system.

03

Glow

Stored energy is gradually emitted as visible light after the source is removed.

Glow duration and brightness vary with pigment chemistry, exposure, concentration, substrate, and conditions. Specific product claims require testing.
