Our technology

Engineered to absorb light. Designed to shine after dark.

Our materials development focuses on strontium aluminate photoluminescent pigments and potential combinations with reflective glass microspheres and compatible coating systems.

We explore solutions that could improve passive visibility in challenging environments, with performance evaluated through application-specific testing.

Product performance, durability, environmental suitability, and regulatory compliance require validation for each intended use.
